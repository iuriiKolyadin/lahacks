# Test clip sources

All converted to 16 kHz mono WAV with ffmpeg. Used only for evaluating the
classifier; not shipped in the app.

- `alarm_*`, `siren_*`, `knock_*`, `horn_*`, `dog_*`, `noise_*` (vacuum
  cleaner), `noise2_*` (crying baby): ESC-50 dataset, K. J. Piczak,
  https://github.com/karolpiczak/ESC-50, CC BY-NC 3.0.
- `doorbell_1..6`: Wikimedia Commons files Doorbell-classic-dingdong.ogg,
  Doorbell-cheap-dingdong.ogg, Electronic doorbell.ogg, Doorbell.wav,
  Doorbell-old-tring.ogg, Mechanical doorbel.ogg, under their respective
  Creative Commons licenses.
- `../plugin/recordings/alarm_1.wav`: recorded through the G2 by Wendy,
  Sat 2026-10-17.
