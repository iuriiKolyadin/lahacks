How it works

The glasses listen and draw. YAMNet runs on a phone.

Every half second the iPhone scores the last second of audio and the server picks one state: dark, cue, speech, name, or alarm. Alarm wins and holds. A name beats speech. Cues last four seconds.

Direction costs no hardware. The SDK carries an undocumented int16 on every audio frame. 322 frames from a real alarm, none empty. The values fit a two mic time difference, so left and right are real and front and back are not.

The G2 stream is tuned for voices and buried under rumble below 300 Hz. A 600 Hz high pass is the only way the real smoke alarm registers, and it erases every knock. Running the model twice per window, filtered and raw, keeps both. Repro: tools/eval.py --dual
