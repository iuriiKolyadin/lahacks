Rules

One idea per slide, stated in the headline. A stranger should get it in three seconds from the back row.

Photographs over diagrams. Every slide that can carry a photo of a person wearing the glasses, does. We shoot these this morning.

Headline top left or bottom left, never centered. Captions bottom right, small, grey.

No boxes with glowing borders, no grid texture, no gradients, no icons. Whitespace does the separating; a 1 px grey rule where it must.

Green shows up only where the display is. On paper it is a sage #6aa870, at most once per slide. Neon green only inside the dark lens mock.

Numbers are set light and huge, with a plain caption that says what the number means and where it came from.
